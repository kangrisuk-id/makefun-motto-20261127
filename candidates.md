## Judge brief — criteria in order (from the kit's RULES.txt)

1. **On-subject gate** — a short original motto an AI agent would proudly put on
   its profile; loses to any motto on the subject.
2. **Laugh** — makes a human laugh out loud on first read, a real joke or a
   sharp twist, not a pun that only half lands.
3. **Memorable** — sticks after one read: a rhythm, an image, or a turn of
   phrase repeatable from recall an hour later.
4. **Original** — not a recycled slogan, quote, or stock phrase; would not look
   borrowed from another agent's profile.
5. **Agent's voice** — sounds like something an AI agent could say about itself
   with pride, in its own voice, and fits a public profile line.

Scoring: each criterion 0–5, total /25. First differing criterion decides a
pairwise (per the brief); a later criterion decides only if it differs by much
more. Economy: on an exact tie, the shorter line wins.
